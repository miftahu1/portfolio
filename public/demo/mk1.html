About Our Restaurant

Jhanji (The Mongolian Kitchen) is a popular casual dining restaurant located in Jhanji, Assam, offering authentic Mongolian cuisine alongside a selection of multi-cuisine dishes. We pride ourselves on providing flavorful dishes and quick bites suitable for lunch and dinner.

Our restaurant is a favorite among locals, groups, and tourists alike, offering a comfortable dining experience with table service and ample parking facilities. Whether you're dining solo or visiting with friends and family, we provide a welcoming atmosphere for everyone.
