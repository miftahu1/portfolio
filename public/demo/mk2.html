A Taste of Mongolia in Assam

Jhanji (The Mongolian Kitchen) is a casual dining restaurant offering flavorful dishes and quick bites, suitable for lunch and dinner. We are popular for solo dining as well as group visits, and are frequently visited by tourists exploring the beautiful region of Assam.

Our restaurant provides a comfortable dining experience with table service and ample parking facilities. Whether you're looking for a quick bite or a full meal, our diverse menu offers something for everyone.
