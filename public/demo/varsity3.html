About Our Café

A Trendy Hangout Spot in Joysagar

The Varsity Cafe is a casual and trendy café located in Joysagar Chariali, Sibsagar, Assam. We're known for our great coffee and tea selection, making us a popular hangout spot for solo diners and groups alike.

Our café provides a comfortable space to relax, work, or socialize with quality food and beverages in a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Whether you're a student looking for a study spot, a family wanting to enjoy a meal together, or friends catching up, we've got the perfect environment for you.
