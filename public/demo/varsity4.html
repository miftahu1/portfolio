About Our Café

The Varsity Cafe is a casual and trendy café located in Joysagar Chariali, Sibsagar, known for its great coffee and tea selection. It's a popular hangout spot for solo diners and groups, offering a comfortable space to relax, work, or socialize.

We provide quality food and beverages with a warm and welcoming atmosphere, making it suitable for families and kids as well. Our café offers multiple service options including dine-in, takeaway, delivery, and drive-through for your convenience.
