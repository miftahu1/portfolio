About Our Cafe

The Varsity Cafe is a premium yet cozy café located in the heart of Joysagar Chariali, Sibsagar, Assam. We've created a welcoming space that's perfect for students, groups, and casual dining.

Our atmosphere is casual and trendy, making us a popular hangout spot for solo diners and groups alike. With free street parking available, we're easily accessible for everyone.

We take pride in offering quality food and beverages with a warm and welcoming atmosphere, making us suitable for families and kids as well.
