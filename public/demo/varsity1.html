Our Story

More Than Just a Café

Welcome to The Varsity Cafe, your cozy hangout spot nestled in the heart of Joysagar, Sibsagar. We're more than just a café – we're a community space where students, young professionals, and food lovers come together to enjoy great food, delicious drinks, and good company.

Our journey began with a simple idea: create a space where people can unwind, connect, and enjoy quality food at reasonable prices. Today, we're proud to be a favorite spot for locals and visitors alike.

Join us on weekends for our special jam nights featuring local talent, or drop by anytime for our carefully crafted menu that blends local flavors with café classics. At The Varsity Cafe, every visit feels like coming home.
